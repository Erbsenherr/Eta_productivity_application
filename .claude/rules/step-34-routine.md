### Step 34: the Routine-Modus, and a morning that is one

Four items from `update.txt`. Database **version 23**. Two of them are one line
each: the **Einstellungen tab is the last one** (`EtaTab` order — tabs are saved by
name, so nothing else moved), and `BlockEditDialog` says under "In die Woche
kopieren" that it makes a further instance and leaves this one standing.

#### Routine-Modus

`Item.routineMode` says a task's steps are worked through **in order, one at a
time**. It changes exactly one thing: what the "Gerade" box draws.

- **`RoutineSteps`** (in `DashboardBoxes.kt`) shows "Schritt n von m", the step that
  is due with its checkbox **behind** it, and nothing that comes after.
  `routineProgress` in `Subtasks.kt` decides which that is: the first step in list
  order that is not ticked.
- **The timestamp is `SubtaskCheck.checkedAt`**, which every tick has always
  carried — no new column for it. `SubtaskRepository.observeCheckTimes` hands it to
  the dashboard, and `DashboardUiState.checked` is now derived from that one flow.
- **The step before stays on one line**, "✓ Duschen · 07:12", with "zurücknehmen"
  beside it. Not in the note, and deliberate: it is the only place the time can be
  read, and the only way to take back a tick made by mistake once the step itself
  has left the screen.
- On the **"Als Nächstes"** page it reads "Routine · 5 Schritte" and offers no
  checkbox, for the reason the ordinary steps are read-only there.
- **Everything else is unchanged**: the planner still draws all the steps on the
  block, "Gruppe erledigt" still confirms the whole task, the evening still carries
  open steps over. A routine is a way of *showing* steps, not a second kind of step.
- **It travels in `ItemExtras`** and is asked inside the Subtasks fold of the Extras
  box (`SubtaskSection`), so every form that can give a card steps can switch it on.
  **Known limit:** the planner's block dialog edits steps through `saveSubtasks`,
  which writes no extras — the switch is not offered there.

#### The Morgenroutine

"Morgenzeit" is now **"Morgenroutine"** (`SetupLabels.MORNING`), generated with
`routineMode = true`. `isMorningRoutine(id)` names its rows: `setup:morning`, or
one `setup:morning-<day>` per night when the weekend has its own.

- **Hour and length still hang off the night** and are regenerated by
  `saveSettings`, which is why the task stays `isOwnedBySettings` and its window on
  the Listen tab still does not offer "Bearbeiten".
- **The steps are the user's**, and are the one thing about it edited in two places,
  as asked: `MorningRoutineBox` in the settings and the task's window on the Listen
  tab. Both mount `MorningRoutineSteps` (in `SubtaskBuilder.kt`) and both save
  through `SetupRepository.saveMorningSteps`, which writes the list onto **every**
  morning definition. With a weekend night the Listen tab shows the weekdays and the
  weekend as two rows; it is still one routine.
- **Saved at once**, not with "Einrichtung sichern": the steps are rows of their
  own, not part of the draft, and the builder's own Sichern is the commit point.
- **`saveSettings` carries the steps across a row swap.** Switching the weekend
  night on or off replaces one daily row by seven or the reverse; the steps are read
  off the live rows *before* the upsert and written onto the new ones after.
- **The builder hides its name field** here (`showName = false`): the name is the
  app's, and a rename would be undone by the next save of the settings.
- The migration renames only rows still called "Morgenzeit" and switches the mode
  on for every morning row. A morning with no steps is simply a block, as before.

#### A bug this turned up: steps on a multi-weekday task

`SubtaskRepository.save` created a new row under the **draft's own id**. A standing
task is one definition per weekday, the form holds the steps of the first, and
`saveGroup` saves that list onto each — so on the second weekday the upsert hit an
existing primary key and **moved** Monday's rows to Tuesday. After one edit only the
last weekday still had steps. `matchedTo` (in `Subtasks.kt`, pinned by
`RoutineTest`) now decides which row a draft lands on: its own id only if that row
belongs to this card, otherwise the unclaimed row of the same name — which keeps
that weekday's ticks — otherwise a fresh id. New rows never take a draft's id.
